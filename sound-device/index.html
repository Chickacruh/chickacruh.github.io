Sound Device · User Research, Model Making, CMFT

JBC Fresh

A rugged portable speaker concept developed through form studies, user feedback, and an appearance model.

Project
Sound Device
Date
November 2024
Designer
Andrew Daugherty
01

Designed for the outdoors.

Group market research informed an individual concept for an outdoor audience. The brief emphasized a portable, rugged-looking speaker with manufacturing efficiency and affordability as design goals. The work shown is a design study and appearance model.

02

Sketching a rugged design language.

Thirty ideation sketches explored how a portable sound device could look and feel. Two sketch models helped compare cylindrical and rectilinear directions before ten further sketches developed the chosen form.

03

Refining the chamfer.

Two more refined models were used to gather feedback. People responded to the chamfered form and suggested that it should also stand on the angled face, which became a direction for further development.

04

Proportion and surface detail.

The front was developed around a root-five rectangle, with golden-ratio relationships guiding the side and chamfer. Grille borders, the clip, buttons, and indicator lights were coordinated with a proportional grid.

05

From drawing to appearance model.

A concept drawing and orthographic views resolved the form before the final appearance model. Dark surfaces, chamfered edges, and orange accents express the intended outdoor character.

06

A portable outdoor companion.

Context photographs and presentation imagery communicate the intended use while hiking and during other outdoor activities. The model communicates the design intent rather than verified speaker performance or durability.
