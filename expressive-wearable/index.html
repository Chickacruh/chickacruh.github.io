Expressive Wearable · Electronics · Prototyping

Groove Pants

An expressive wearable that translates movement into light, bringing a little more personality to the dance floor.

Project
Expressive Wearable
Duration
About one month
Process
Ideation, electronics, garment prototyping
01

Thirty ideas. Three directions.

With about a month to develop an expressive wearable, I started with 30 ideation sketches. Peer review helped me narrow the work to three concepts: a mischievous moustache, a happy-to-sad tail, and the Groove Pants.

02

Making the electronics wearable.

I built a breadboard model to test the electronics, then checked how they would fit inside the pants. Alongside the fit tests, I experimented with materials to diffuse the LEDs into a more continuous line of light.

03

Refining the fit.

The first electronics enclosure looked boxy when worn. I added padding and shaped the enclosure to give it a more natural profile, then tested the assembly in a pair of pants that suited the concept.

04

From test model to finished garment.

After proving the concept, I attached the LED strip and diffusion layer more securely. Further code adjustments improved how the pants reacted to movement.

05

Light that moves with you.

The finished Groove Pants turn movement into light. When standing still, the pants pulse blue. Poses send an animation down the legs, while spinning produces a rainbow that becomes brighter and faster as the movement speeds up.
