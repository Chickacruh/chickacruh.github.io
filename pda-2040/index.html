PDA 2040 · Concept Design, Sketching, Model Making

Mind Link Wrist

A speculative wrist-worn personal digital assistant exploring electromyography as an input method.

Project
PDA 2040
Date
October 2024
Designer
Andrew Daugherty
01

Imagining a personal digital device.

PDA 2040 explores a future personal digital assistant worn on the wrist. The Mind Link Wrist concept considers electromyography as an input method, with the intent of offering an alternative to an invasive implant. This project develops the concept and physical form; it does not demonstrate a working EMG interface.

02

More than one hundred starting points.

Quick sketches explored devices around the hands and head. Three rough models tested a head-mounted option and two wristband approaches. Pocketability helped move the direction toward the wrist.

03

Developing the wristband.

Further sketches refined the wrist-worn form. Faceted models explored a flexible band, moving from tape-connected studies toward a more detailed proposal for how the strap could bend and attach.

04

Communicating material and construction.

The final model used acrylic, chipboard, and rubber to represent the intended glass, plastic, and metal components. A cut rubber band let the connection sit flat, while added detail made the strap and facets more legible.

05

A concept made tangible.

The model sequence records the move from quick form studies to the final appearance model. The finished model and desk presentation bring the concept, material choices, and development process together.
