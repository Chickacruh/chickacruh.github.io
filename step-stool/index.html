Step Stool · Sketching, CAD, Woodworking

Step + Stow

A two-step stool that stays in view as a bookshelf and display piece.

Project
Step Stool
Date
Spring 2025
Team
Sarah Ahmed & Andrew Daugherty
01

A step stool worth keeping out.

Our market research found many stools aimed at children or professionals, with similar materials and storage features. We explored a stool that could stay in the living space and display books and meaningful objects. The design targeted a 20 × 20 inch footprint, two 7 inch rises, and space for books below the upper step.

02

From platforms to a corner cut.

Early ideas explored larger platforms and adjustable steps. Sketches and small models led us toward a corner-cut bookshelf form: a way to create more usable storage while keeping a comfortable lower step.

03

Testing the form at three scales.

Quarter-scale, half-scale, and full-scale models helped us understand assembly and stability. An early cantilevered first step was unstable, so it was revised. Handle openings were also explored and later removed because they interrupted the form.

04

Joinery, finish, and material planning.

Cardboard and plywood test pieces checked the joints before final fabrication. We compared one and two coats of stain, choosing the darker two-coat finish. Further revisions unified the joint direction, simplified the supports, and fitted the parts onto a 2 × 4 foot plywood sheet.

05

Building the final stool.

The many smaller parts made glue-up a staged process. Tight corners also complicated staining and clear coating. Additional support pieces were added beneath the step platforms during assembly.

06

Step up. Store. Display.

The final prototype brings the two-step structure and open shelving together in a dark-stained form. These photographs show the studio views and the stool used as a step and a display surface.
